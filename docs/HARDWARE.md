# Hardware status

Last recorded validation: 10 October 2026. Results apply to one development phone.
Working basics does not mean every mode is qualified. Raw device evidence stays local.

| Hardware | Status | Recorded evidence | Remaining |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Display, touch and rotation | Working basics | GPU rendering, touch and rotation user-verified. v20 panel rail timing fixes the repeated black wake screen; user confirms. | Longer wake/reboot testing; rotation still feels a little sluggish. |
| CPU and GPU | Working basics | All 8 CPU cores, stock 2.8032 GHz boost, Adreno 630 rendering and 120 Hz presentation verified. | Sustained load, thermal throttling and battery-cost measurements. |
| Wi-Fi | Working; throughput needs work | Scanning, connection, internet and authorized debugging work. Local synthetic SSH transfers remain slow; a 1 MiB test completed at 1.22 Mbit/s toward the PC and 1.65 Mbit/s toward the phone, including SSH startup. Power-save disable did not help; original setting restored. Connection recovered after the two-minute sleep. | Investigate throughput, factory MAC, roaming and repeated suspend recovery. Advertised monitor mode has not been functionally tested. |
| Bluetooth | Partial | User paired and connected a peripheral; discovery and power-on work. | Controller sleep workaround remains. Audio profiles and reconnect after sleep need testing. |
| Speakers and microphone | Working basics | User heard a clear speaker tone; microphone captured acoustic tone on both PCM channels and desktop recording passed. | Voice quality, startup transients, channel independence and headset/USB audio remain unvalidated. |
